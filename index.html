An interactive workbench for 3D processing and reconstruction experiments. Supports DICOM loading, three-plane MPR, multi-organ segmentation, ROI measurements and project save/restore. Checks series identity and spatial geometry to prevent misplaced masks and stale cache reuse.

Implemented direct Fourier and several iterative CT reconstruction methods, with residual U-Net experiments for sparse-view reconstruction. Correcting missing spacing resampling improved mean Dice from 0.684 to 0.840 in a historical paired comparison of 20 cases from the same public data source.

The Dice comparison and the reconstruction screenshot are separate experiments. These results are not independent clinical validation. The software is for education and research.

Implementation & validation

A competition project for endoscopic classification with few positive samples. The task objective is precision at 90% recall. Uses GastroNet-pretrained ResNet-50, class sampling, EMA, near-duplicate grouping and bidirectional leave-one-center-out evaluation.

Aligned training and inference preprocessing, averaged probabilities from 12 random-seed models and applied horizontal-flip augmentation. Packaged inference in Docker with time-budget scheduling, timeout fallback and input/output checks.

Internal LOCO evaluation supported method selection; it is not an independent test result for the final submitted weights. No competition ranking is claimed.

Implementation & validation

A runnable audit workflow for leakage, near-duplicates and feature attributes. The configuration-driven CLI supports cross-split near-duplicate checks, patient-group leakage checks and overall/within-class attribute probes. Metric functions include AUROC, Brier score, ECE and patient-cluster bootstrap.

Includes an English tutorial, running guide and runnable synthetic examples. The companion work From Pixels to Patients was a finalist in the MICCAI Educational Challenge 2026.

Predictable attributes do not establish that the classifier relies on them. Synthetic examples are not clinical performance evidence. The full calibration audit is not yet integrated into the CLI.
