LoRA Fine-Tuning of π0.5 on LIBERO

OpenPI · JAX · LoRA · LIBERO-Spatial · Nov 2026

I fine-tuned π0.5 with LoRA on one RTX A5000 to pick and place objects using camera images and language instructions. Under the same evaluation protocol on 400 fixed held-out initial states across 10 LIBERO-Spatial simulation tasks, the policy succeeded in 0 episodes before fine-tuning and 382 (95.5%) after 2,000 updates.

Release v1.1.0 / Evaluation results / Matched rollout video

Implementation & validation

Each training example paired scene and wrist images and an instruction with the next 10 demonstrated actions for flow matching. I split trajectories into 1,608 training and 85 offline validation demonstrations and fit normalization on training data. With OpenPI pi05_base frozen, I configured rank-64 LoRA for 456 linear matrices across the vision tower, language backbone, action expert and related projections. Accumulating 32 microbatches of eight gave an effective batch of 256. I restored the model, optimizer, sampler and random state to continue training to 2,000 updates.

The policy predicted 10 actions at a time and executed five before observing again. Across 399 pairs with identical first observations, the task-weighted gain over step zero was 95.50 percentage points (95% paired interval [93.50, 97.25]). Of six checkpoints, step 1,250 scored highest on the original inference seed (389/400). Its mean lead over the endpoint on two additional seeds was 1.25 percentage points (95% interval [−0.50, 2.875]).

Of the 18 initial states on which the endpoint failed, 16 succeeded on at least one additional inference seed. The release includes 5,100 scored outcomes, analysis scripts and matched rollout videos. Results cover one training run and ten known simulation tasks.

Camera Input Ablation in VLA Policies

LeRobot · OpenPI · MuJoCo · LIBERO · VLA

On the same 500 initial states, masking the wrist camera reduced success from 98.4% to 4.6% for LeRobot π0.5 v044 and from 72.0% to 71.2% for π0 v044. I also found that when one camera input was missing, LeRobot placed subsequent images in the wrong input slots.

Code & results / Dashboard

Implementation & validation

Normal input, wrist masking and wrist first-frame freezing each used 500 episodes (10 tasks × 50 initial states), with 492, 23 and 0 successes. Masking minus normal was −93.8 percentage points, with a paired 95% interval of [−96.0, −91.6]. The corresponding π0 v044 rates were 72.0%, 71.2% and 72.8%. The main view kept updating.

I scoped the fix, reviewed the changes and submitted a patch covering six LeRobot policies. Of 27 new regression tests, 21 failed on the original implementation and all 27 passed after the fix. PR #5129 is not yet merged.

Added an official π0/OpenPI comparison on 100 matched initial-state indices: official π0 scored 93/100 normally and 0/100 under blackout; LeRobot π0 v044 scored 65/100 normally and 53–56/100 in five blackout repeats. State-freezing controls support sensitivity to state updates; selected fixed-action replay succeeded on 262/400 selection-excluded states. Episode records, analysis tables, protocol audits and CI recomputation are archived in the v1.5.3 evidence freeze.

Results are checkpoint- and protocol-specific, not a uniform model ranking. The five π0 blackout repeats reuse the same 100 initial states. Official and LeRobot comparisons change both weights and pipelines, so differences cannot be uniquely attributed to conversion or training. First-frame freezing also disrupts image–state synchronization; replay success does not identify the policy’s internal mechanism. The separate fine-tuning project is outside this freeze.

3D CT Segmentation and Reconstruction Workstation

PySide6 · NumPy · ONNX Runtime · CT Reconstruction · 2026.05–2026.08

I built a desktop CT workstation for reconstruction, organ segmentation and ROI measurements. Correcting voxel-spacing resampling in the segmentation pipeline raised mean Dice from 0.684 to 0.840 in a historical paired comparison of 20 cases from the same public data source.

Code & documentation

Implementation & validation

The workstation supports DICOM loading, three-plane MPR, multi-organ segmentation, ROI measurements and project save/restore. Checks series identity and spatial geometry to prevent misplaced masks and stale cache reuse.

Implemented direct Fourier and several iterative CT reconstruction methods, with residual U-Net experiments for sparse-view reconstruction. Segmentation Dice improved in all 20 paired cases.

The Dice comparison and the reconstruction screenshot are separate experiments. These results are not independent clinical validation. The software is for education and research.

RARE26: Cross-Center Endoscopic Image Classification and Ensemble Inference

PyTorch · ResNet-50 · GastroNet · Docker

I evaluated endoscopic image classifiers across two centers and packaged a 12-model ensemble in Docker. On the challenge platform’s T4 GPU, the full ensemble completed a 384-frame inference check in 76.1 seconds without triggering the fallback.

Code & methods

Implementation & validation

Positive cases were rare. The target metric was precision at 90% recall. Uses GastroNet-pretrained ResNet-50, class sampling, EMA, near-duplicate grouping and bidirectional leave-one-center-out evaluation.

Aligned training and inference preprocessing, averaged probabilities from 12 random-seed models and applied horizontal-flip augmentation. Packaged inference in Docker with time-budget scheduling, timeout fallback and input/output checks.

Internal LOCO evaluation supported method selection; it is not an independent test result for the final submitted weights. No competition ranking is claimed.
